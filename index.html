Our flagship · Samarahan, Sarawak

Local foundation. Phased expansion.

WCH is developing a proposed hybrid compute facility in the Samarahan industrial area. The initial 2 MW phase is planned for Mini-PC hosting and Bitcoin mining, with future AI/HPC and third-party hosting options subject to demand and technical readiness.

2 MWPlanned initial phase
10 MWProposed power framework
10 yearsNegotiated supply term

Project is at development stage. Utility and land rights, permitted use, engineering, financing and commissioning remain subject to documentation and approvals. MW figures refer to proposed site intake, not commissioned IT load.
